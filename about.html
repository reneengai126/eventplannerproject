How it works

Events get canceled over paperwork, not ideas.

Student organizations planning on-campus events can't easily find the forms, approvals and deadlines they need. First-time officers inherit the role with little context, and the knowledge leaves with graduating seniors. EventPlanner AI gives every RSO president, events chair and treasurer a complete first draft of their event in under a minute.

Money

Funding requests get cut for missing a deadline or a small required detail.

Time

Hours per event spent asking offices which forms apply — and procedures keep changing.

Opportunity

Events move or cancel because rooms were booked late or catering wasn't from an approved vendor.

Continuity

Each new board relearns the same rules from scratch.

What happens when you hit "Generate"

  1. Parse the prompt. Headcount, budget, date and time are pulled from your sentence; the event type (gala, panel, workshop, social, festival, performance, competition, fundraiser) is detected from keywords.
  2. Instant plan. A template engine builds the budget, run of show, staffing, checklist, emails, supply list and floor map, scaled to your headcount. This always works, even offline.
  3. Local AI pass. If Ollama is running, the model returns structured JSON (theme, palette, attendee journey, run of show, event-specific costs, supplies and tasks). Every value is validated before it's merged — unrealistic prices are dropped.
  4. AI-written emails. The member invitation and VIP/leadership email are written for your event and streamed into the editor. Any email can be rewritten with a different tone or instructions.

Guardrails for accuracy

  • The AI is told never to invent university policies, deadlines or vendor names.
  • Checklist items that depend on official policy are tagged Verify with SGS/CSLE.
  • All prices are labeled estimates; the budget is a live spreadsheet so treasurers can drop in real quotes.
  • Everything stays editable and exportable (CSV, .ics calendar, JSON, print/PDF).

Known gaps we're testing

  • Maintenance: policies and vendor lists change yearly — someone has to own updates.
  • Endorsement: SGS/CSLE may not want an unofficial tool giving advice.
  • Unwritten rules that aren't in any document.
  • Adoption: will officers switch from asking offices in person?

Set up the local AI (Ollama)

  1. Install Ollama from ollama.com/download (Windows, Mac, Linux).
  2. Pull a model in a terminal:
    ollama pull llama3.2
    Smaller/faster: phi4-mini, gemma3:4b. Better quality: llama3.1:8b, qwen2.5:7b.
  3. Start this site by double-clicking Start-EventPlanner.bat. It serves the site at http://localhost:8080 — Ollama only accepts requests from localhost pages, not files opened directly.
  4. Check the status pill in the top-right corner. Green means connected; click it to choose a model.
